    How these figures were produced

    Availability dates, starting locations and travel notes are taken from the submitted availability sheet. Game dates, teams and venues are taken from the published NAHL and USHL schedules for the same period.

    • A game counts for a date only if that date was submitted as available.
    • "Within driving distance" means the home rink is one of the teams listed as local to the starting location recorded for that date.
    • "Max number of Games Per Trip" applies one game per day, and two on a day offering two or more showcase games.
    • Local means under 5 hours' driving from the starting location recorded for that date. "Local or Flight" marks a trip that had both; "Local only" had no game beyond that range; "Flight only" had none inside it.
    • A "drivable combination" is a group of rinks that are all within about 300 road miles (roughly 5 hours) of one another, so any two of them can be reached by car on consecutive days. Showcase dates are included in this count.
    • Every figure on this page can be traced to a single row in the accompanying workbook.
