    How these figures were produced

    Availability dates, starting locations and travel notes are taken from the submitted availability sheet. Game dates, teams and venues are taken from the published NAHL and USHL schedules for the same period.

    • A game counts for a date only if that date was submitted as available.
    • "Within driving distance" means the home rink is one of the teams listed as local to the starting location recorded for that date.
    • "Most workable" applies one game per day, and two on a day offering two or more showcase games.
    • Every figure on this page can be traced to a single row in the accompanying workbook.
